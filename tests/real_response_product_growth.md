The growth review does not fit a simple funnel or timeline. Three signals point in different directions.

Activation is improving for teams that complete the guided setup, but fewer new accounts reach that step because the first screen asks for too much configuration. Expansion revenue is strongest in workspaces that invite three or more collaborators in the first week. Support volume is rising around permissions, which suggests that collaboration is valuable but the admin model is not obvious.

The best next move is not a single feature launch. Treat the problem as an insight map: simplify first-run setup, move the collaborator invitation earlier, add permission presets, and instrument the setup-to-collaboration path. The main risk is over-optimizing activation while making admin trust worse. The strongest evidence to watch is time to first shared workspace, invited collaborators per account, and permission-related ticket rate.
